**Subject: Request for permission and bulk / API access to published Award of Contract (AOC) data on the Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP)**

Sir / Madam,

1. We, [Company name], [constitution, e.g. a private limited company / proprietorship / LLP] registered at [registered address] ([CIN / Udyam Registration No.]), are developing a procurement-analytics service that helps suppliers, particularly MSMEs, prepare for government tenders in good time. The service shows which public contracts are approaching their published completion date, so that eligible suppliers can plan to participate when the requirement is re-tendered.

2. The service relies on Award of Contract information that buyer organisations already publish on CPPP under "Result of Tenders": organisation name, tender ID and reference number, tender description, contract date, contract value, number of bids received, name(s) of the selected bidder(s), and date of completion / completion period.

3. The CPPP Copyright Policy states that material featured on the Portal may be reproduced free of charge after taking proper permission. We are therefore writing to request:

   a. **permission** to use and display published AOC information in our service, with the source prominently acknowledged; and

   b. a **sanctioned means of access** that does not require automated use of the public search page. That page is protected by a CAPTCHA, which we will not attempt to bypass. Any one of the following would meet our needs:
      - a periodic (daily or weekly) bulk export of newly published AOC records;
      - read-only API access, with whatever rate limits and credentials the Division prescribes; or
      - publication of AOC data as a dataset on the Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in), in line with the National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP), 2012.

4. In using the data, we undertake to:
   - reproduce it accurately, without alteration, and never in a derogatory manner or a misleading context;
   - prominently acknowledge CPPP and the respective buyer organisation as the source, and identify each record by its Tender ID so that users can verify it on the Portal;
   - clearly label our own analysis (for example, the time remaining until a published completion date) as analysis, not as official information;
   - not present any re-tender as certain, and direct users to the official Portal for all tender notices and bidding;
   - comply with any conditions, rate limits, attribution format or usage restrictions the Division or NIC prescribe, and discontinue use promptly if permission is withdrawn; and
   - use no personal data beyond what is already published in AOC records.

5. We believe wider, structured access to award information supports the objectives of transparency and competition in public procurement, and of increased MSME participation under the Public Procurement Policy for Micro and Small Enterprises.

6. We would be glad to provide any further information, a demonstration of the service, or to meet officers of the Division or NIC at their convenience.
